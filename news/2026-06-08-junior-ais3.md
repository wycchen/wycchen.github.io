Registration is open for **Junior AIS3**, the 2026 cybersecurity course for high school and vocational high school students, organized under the Ministry of Education's Cross-disciplinary Information Security Talent Cultivation Program.

- **Registration**: Jun. 8 (Mon.) 10:00 – Jul. 6 (Mon.) 22:00, 2026 (submit screening materials together with your registration)
- **Admission list announced**: by Jul. 13 (Mon.) 20:00, 2026
- **Summer course**: Aug. 4 (Tue.) – Aug. 7 (Fri.), 2026
- **Topics**: web security / AI security / introduction to CTF
- **Venue**: Room 104, Delta Building, National Tsing Hua University (No. 101, Sec. 2, Guangfu Rd., East Dist., Hsinchu City)

Open to enrolled students who are ROC nationals and have basic information security skills.

Contact: +886-2-2771-2171 ext. 4278, [ais3moe@gmail.com](mailto:ais3moe@gmail.com)
