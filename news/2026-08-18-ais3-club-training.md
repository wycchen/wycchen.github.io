The 2026 AIS3 Club officer training has been expanded with sessions in both northern and central Taiwan, so clubs can join whichever region suits them.

**Northern session — Sat., Sep. 12, 2026**

- Time: 9:30–17:00
- Venue: GIS Taipei Tech Convention Center
- Program: community sharing, CTF challenge design and write-up sharing, regional networking

**Central session — Sat., Sep. 19, 2026**

- Time: 9:30–17:00
- Venue: GIS Wuri Convention Center (Taichung)
- Program: community sharing, designing and running cybersecurity outreach activities, regional networking

Please register via the QR code on the official event poster.
